# 🍺 Flappy Beer

A funny Flappy Bird clone where you flap a googly-eyed beer mug through the taps.
Every tap you clear, you chug another beer — and the drunker you get, the more the
whole screen wobbles. Don't spill.

## Play

Just open the file — no build, no dependencies:

```bash
open index.html      # macOS
```

Or double-click `index.html`.

## Controls

- **Click / tap / SPACE** — flap
- Clear a tap → **CHUG!** (+1 score, +BAC)
- The **BAC meter** (top-left) fills as you score; the world wobbles harder as it rises
- Hit a tap, the floor, or the ceiling → **you're cut off** (click to retry)

Best score is saved locally in your browser.

## Please drink responsibly 🍻
It's just a mug with googly eyes, but still.
