RE: Custom Foot Orthotics — Denied

Dear Member,

We have completed our review of the above claim, submitted for a pair of
custom-fabricated foot orthotics billed under HCPCS code L3000 (foot
orthotic, custom fabricated, includes fitting and adjustment).

This claim has been DENIED.

Reason for denial:
The procedure code submitted does not correspond to a covered benefit as
documented. HCPCS L3000 was billed as a custom-fabricated device; however,
the clinical documentation and the diagnosis code submitted with the claim
do not establish medical necessity for a CUSTOM-fabricated orthotic over a
prefabricated (over-the-counter) device under Meridian Durable Medical
Equipment Policy DME-14.3 (Foot Orthotics and Orthopedic Footwear).

Per policy DME-14.3, coverage of a custom-fabricated foot orthotic (L3000)
requires (a) a qualifying diagnosis, and (b) clinical documentation of a
foot deformity or condition that cannot be accommodated by a prefabricated
device. The submitted record did not include documentation distinguishing
the need for custom fabrication, and the claim was therefore processed as
a non-covered item.

Your appeal rights:
You may appeal this decision. A written appeal must be received within 180
days of the date of this notice. If your internal appeal is denied, you
may have the right to an Independent Medical Review through the California
Department of Insurance.
