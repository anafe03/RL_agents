Patient is a 67-year-old female, three weeks post right total knee arthroplasty. She reports pain in her right knee 4 out of 10 at rest and 6 out of 10 with stairs. Functional limitation: still struggling with stair descent. Her goals are to return to walking her dog and gardening within six weeks.

On exam, right knee flexion is 110 degrees, extension lacks 5 degrees. Left knee within normal limits. Right quadriceps strength is 3 out of 5, hamstring 4 out of 5. Mild effusion still present. Gait shows decreased right stance time and reduced terminal knee extension at toe-off.

Assessment: she is progressing as expected post-op and is on track with ROM goals, but quadriceps strength remains the limiting factor. Continue therapeutic exercise focus on quad activation and ROM.

For her home program: terminal knee extensions, three sets of fifteen, twice daily. Heel slides, three sets of ten. Quad sets, hold five seconds, three sets of ten, twice daily. We will see her twice a week for the next four weeks.
