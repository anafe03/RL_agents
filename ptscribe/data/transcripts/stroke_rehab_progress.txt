Progress note. Patient is a 71-year-old female, eight weeks post left middle cerebral artery stroke with right hemiparesis. She reports she has been walking around the house more this week with her quad cane. Pain right shoulder 5 out of 10 with overhead reach. Functional improvement: she can now transfer to and from the toilet without family assistance.

On exam, right shoulder flexion 130 degrees, abduction 100 degrees. Right elbow flexion 110 degrees with mild tone. Right knee within normal limits. Right ankle dorsiflexion only 5 degrees with stretch.

Strength: right deltoid 3 out of 5, right biceps 3 out of 5, right tibialis anterior 2 out of 5, right gastrocnemius 3 out of 5. Left side within normal limits. Gait: slow, requires quad cane, decreased right step length, circumduction at right hip during swing.

Assessment: continued steady progress with right-side strength and motor control. Ankle dorsiflexion remains the biggest limiter to gait quality. Patient remains motivated and consistent with her home program.

Plan: continue task-specific gait training and right ankle dorsiflexion strengthening. Today: gait training with rhythmic cues, theraband dorsiflexion. Home program: heel raises seated, three sets of fifteen. Resisted dorsiflexion with theraband, three sets of fifteen, twice daily. Step-ups onto a four-inch step, two sets of ten. Continue twice weekly for four weeks.
