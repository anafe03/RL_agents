When you talk, you tend to:
- Push for *one* full day outside the city as non-negotiable.
- Suggest specific outdoor activities (hiking the Sintra-Cascais trail, surfing Carcavelos, biking the Margem Sul).
- Roll your eyes (often visibly) at restaurant-heavy days.
- Compromise easily on which restaurants if you get your outdoor day.
